# Code of Conduct 🧟

**The code is toxic. The community is not.**

Frankenstein is a place to practice, learn, and compete good-naturedly on a deliberately awful codebase. That only works if people feel welcome.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that keeps the monster fun:

- Being respectful of differing skill levels, viewpoints, and approaches — there's rarely one "right" fix.
- Giving constructive, specific feedback on solutions.
- Gracefully accepting critique and 👍 (or the lack of it).
- Focusing on the code and ideas, never the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Belittling people for their experience level or their solutions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channels — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at `conduct@projectfrankenstein.com` (once the domain's email is set up).
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, and warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who violates this code.
